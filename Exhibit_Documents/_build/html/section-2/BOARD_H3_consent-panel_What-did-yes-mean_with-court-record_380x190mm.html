WHAT DID “YES” MEAN?
Three versions of one permission · 1990–1994
1. What tribal leaders were told
Diabetes research
The project was described to the Tribal Council again and again as a study of diabetes.
2. What the written form said
“…to study the causes of behavioral/medical disorders.”
The consent form signed by some of the early donors.
3. What later donors heard
Diabetes research
From 1991 to 1994 some blood was taken with spoken, not written, consent, explained as diabetes research.
