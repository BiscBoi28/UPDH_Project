It took decades
1876–1920, drawn to scale
28 quiet years
1876
Wickham’s 70,000 seeds leave Brazil. No law forbids it.
1884
Amazonas puts high export duties on rubber seeds.
1890s
First commercial rubber plantings in Malaya.
1912
Brazil passes the Rubber Defence Law.
1913
Asian plantations overtake the Amazon.
1918
Brazil bans the export of rubber seeds.
1920
Wickham is knighted in Britain.
Twenty years after the seeds left, Brazil is still the world’s main source of rubber.
