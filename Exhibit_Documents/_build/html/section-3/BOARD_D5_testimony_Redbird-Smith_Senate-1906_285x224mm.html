IN HIS OWN WORDS
“I never consented to any agreement to divide up my land, and I don’t want it divided up. I want the old treaty.”
— Redbird Smith, Cherokee (Keetoowah) leader, testimony to a US Senate select committee, Muskogee, November 1906
Testimony of Redbird Smith
November 1906 to January 1907 · Senate Report 5013, vol. 1, pp. 97–98 · Reproduction
Arrested for refusing to enrol, he told the committee he was ‘made to enroll against my will’. The committee’s answer, on the next page: take your allotment, or ‘you will soon find yourself without land altogether’.
