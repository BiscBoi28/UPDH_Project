Same goods · Two verdicts
Comparison
New York · January 1882
Ellen Sardy
Caught in Simpson, Crawford & Simpson’s Sixth Avenue store with three pairs of stockings and five pieces of silk. Her lawyer admitted the theft and pleaded that she had been ‘of weak mind’.
Kleptomania
She went free.
London · May 1895
Hannah Wilson & Ann Hanstead
Caught at a City silk warehouse with eighty-six yards of silk rolled under a skirt. Wilson told the court she was ‘a respectable woman, of Hackney’. No doctor spoke for them; both had a previous conviction.
Theft
Guilty. Three years’ penal servitude.
A Paris newspaper of the period suggested defining a thief as ‘a kind of kleptomaniac, but of the lower social class’ — and a kleptomaniac as ‘a kind of thief, but of the better class’.
