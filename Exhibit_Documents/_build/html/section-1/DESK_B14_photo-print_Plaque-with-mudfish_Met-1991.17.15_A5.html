Plaque with mudfish
Edo peoples, Benin, c.1540–70 · Brass, 34 × 17.1 cm · Photograph; the original is in The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York (1991.17.15)
The mudfish lives in water and can survive on land, and in Benin it stood for the ọba’s power in both worlds.
