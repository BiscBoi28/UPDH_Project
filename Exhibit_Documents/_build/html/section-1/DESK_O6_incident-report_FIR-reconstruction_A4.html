Statement of Sandeep Kumar Arora S/o Late Shri Ramesh Chand Arora, R/o House No. 214, Pocket C-8, Sector 8, Rohini, Delhi, aged about 41 years.

I reside at the above address and work in a private company. Today, i.e. 14/09/2025, I had come to Chandni Chowk for shopping. At about 4:30 pm I bought a gold ring from a shop in Dariba Kalan and kept it, along with its bill, inside my wallet. I put the wallet in the right back pocket of my trousers. At about 5:30 pm I was walking on Main Chandni Chowk Road towards the Metro station. There was a heavy crowd near the rickshaw stand and I was pushed from behind by some persons. At about 5:45 pm, when I reached near Gate No. 3 of Chandni Chowk Metro Station, I found that my wallet was missing from my back pocket.

My wallet contained cash of about ₹4,300, one SBI debit card, one HDFC credit card, my Aadhaar card, my driving licence and the gold ring with its bill. I searched for the wallet nearby but could not find it. I then called my bank and got both cards blocked. I did not see who took my wallet. Some unknown person has stolen my wallet from my pocket in the crowd. Legal action may kindly be taken.
