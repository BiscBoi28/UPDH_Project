Section 303, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
2023 · The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II — Section 1 · Clipped from the official Gazette
India’s criminal code defines theft as dishonestly taking movable property from someone’s possession without consent. Explanation 1 spells out the limit: a thing attached to the earth ‘is not the subject of theft’. Under this section, land cannot be stolen.
