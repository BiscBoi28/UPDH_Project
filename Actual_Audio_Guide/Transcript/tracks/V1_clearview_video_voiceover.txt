Three photos, posted in three places. A friend's post. A blog. Her own account.
A system copies each one. The originals stay online. Software finds her face, and measures it.
The measurements become numbers, stored among billions of others.
Then, a new photo. A woman on a street. Her face is measured the same way, and compared.
Three matches. Three pages. Clearview AI built a search like this from over one hundred billion images.
